Spanish Proverbs

Spanish ki 24 mashhoor kahawatein — maani aur misaal ke saath.

Proverbs are the soul of a language — short, musical and full of advice people have repeated for centuries. Here are all 24 Spanish proverbs from the app's Proverbs pack, each with its word-for-word translation (so you can see how Spanish builds the image), its real meaning in English, and a Roman Urdu rendering.

ProverbWord for wordMeaningRoman Urdu
A quien madruga, Dios le ayuda.God helps the one who rises early.The early bird catches the worm — early risers succeed.Jo subah jaldi uthta hai, Allah us ki madad karta hai.
Más vale tarde que nunca.Better late than never.Doing something late is better than never doing it.Dair aye durust aye — na karne se dair se karna behtar hai.
No hay mal que por bien no venga.There is no bad thing from which good does not come.Every cloud has a silver lining.Har burai mein koi bhalai chhupi hoti hai.
Ojos que no ven, corazón que no siente.Eyes that do not see, a heart that does not feel.Out of sight, out of mind.Jo nazar na aye, dil ko mehsoos nahi hota.
Más vale pájaro en mano que ciento volando.A bird in the hand is worth more than a hundred flying.A bird in the hand is worth two in the bush.Haath ki ek cheez, hawa mein sau se behtar hai.
A caballo regalado no se le mira el diente.Do not look at the teeth of a horse you were given.Don't look a gift horse in the mouth.Muft ki cheez mein nuqas nahi dhoondte.
El que mucho abarca, poco aprieta.Whoever grabs a lot, squeezes little.Don't try to do too much at once.Jo har cheez pakarne jaye, us ke haath kuch nahi aata.
Dime con quién andas y te diré quién eres.Tell me who you go with and I will tell you who you are.You are judged by the company you keep.Batao kis ke saath rehte ho, bata dunga tum kaun ho — suhbat asar karti hai.
La práctica hace al maestro.Practice makes the master.Practice makes perfect.Mashq hi ustad banati hai.
Querer es poder.To want is to be able.Where there is a will, there is a way.Jahan chaah, wahan raah.
No dejes para mañana lo que puedas hacer hoy.Do not leave for tomorrow what you can do today.Never put off work you can do now.Aaj ka kaam kal par mat chhoro.
Al mal tiempo, buena cara.To bad weather, a good face.Face hard times with courage and a smile.Mushkil waqt mein hosla aur muskurahat rakho.
Más vale prevenir que curar.Better to prevent than to cure.Prevention is better than cure.Ilaaj se parhez behtar hai.
El saber no ocupa lugar.Knowledge takes up no space.You can never learn too much.Ilm ka koi bojh nahi hota.
Poco a poco se va lejos.Little by little, one goes far.Slow and steady wins the race.Qatra qatra hi darya banta hai.
En casa del herrero, cuchillo de palo.In the blacksmith's house, a wooden knife.The people who serve others often go without themselves.Lohar ke ghar mein lakri ka chaaqu — jo doosron ke liye hai, khud mehroom.
A palabras necias, oídos sordos.To foolish words, deaf ears.Ignore nonsense and gossip.Bewaqoof ki baat par kaan mat dharo.
El tiempo es oro.Time is gold.Time is precious — do not waste it.Waqt sona hai, zaya mat karo.
Barriga llena, corazón contento.Full belly, happy heart.A full stomach makes everyone happier.Pait bhara ho to dil khush hota hai.
Año nuevo, vida nueva.New year, new life.A new year is a fresh start.Naya saal, nayi zindagi.
El que ríe último, ríe mejor.Who laughs last, laughs best.Final victory matters more than early success.Jo aakhir mein hansta hai, wahi asal jeetta hai.
Más vale malo conocido que bueno por conocer.Better the known bad than the unknown good.Better the devil you know than the one you don't.Jaani pehchani burai, anjaan bhalai se behtar hoti hai (aksar).
Donde hay humo, hay fuego.Where there is smoke, there is fire.Rumours usually have some truth behind them.Jahan dhuwan hota hai, wahan aag bhi hoti hai.
La unión hace la fuerza.Unity makes strength.Together we are strong.Ittihad mein taqat hai.
