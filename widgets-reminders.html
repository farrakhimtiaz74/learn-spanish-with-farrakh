Widgets & Reminders

Home screen par roz ka lafz aur streak — aur ek reminder jo phone restart par bhi nahi bhoolta.

Teen home-screen widgets

📖 Word of the Day

Har roz ek naya Spanish lafz — maani aur awaz ke saath — seedha home screen par. App khole baghair roz ki ek chhoti si dose.

🔥 Streak Widget

Tumhari streak (lagataar seekhne ke din) home screen par nazar aati hai — ek nazar mein pata chal jata hai ke silsila qaim hai.

📚 Review Due

Aaj kitne cards review ke liye due hain — number widget par. Bhoolne se pehle yaad karane wala chhota sa ishara.

⏰ Daily reminder

Apne pasandeeda waqt par roz ka reminder set karo. Phone band karke dobara kholo (reboot) to reminder khud dobara set ho jata hai — aksar apps yahin fail hoti hain. Progress tab mein reminder ki poori halat (notification on hai? exact time ki ijazat hai?) saaf nazar aati hai, aur kharab ho to wahi se theek bhi ho jati hai.

🔔 Smart notifications

Har roz ek jaisi ghanti nahi: kabhi roz ka lafz, kabhi due review ki yaad, kabhi streak bachane ki narmi se targheeb. Maksad dabao nahi — bas ek halki si yaad-dehani.

🧊 Streak freezes

Zindagi mein ek din choot jaye to streak khatam? Nahi. Har 7 din ki streak par ek streak freeze milta hai (2 tak jama) — ek choota hua din freeze se cover ho jata hai aur silsila nahi tootta.

Hamara usool: consistency dabao se nahi aati. Widgets aur reminders sirf ishara hain — streak ek din toot bhi jaye to seekha hua kahin nahi jata. Kal phir shuru kar lo, alfaz SRS mein tumhara intezar kar rahe honge.
