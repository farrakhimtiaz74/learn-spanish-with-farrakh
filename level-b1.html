B1 Spanish — Intermediate

Ab tum safar aur roz ke mushkil kaam Spanish mein sambhal saktay ho.

What B1 means

Intermediate. You can deal with most situations that come up while travelling, describe experiences, events, dreams and ambitions, and give reasons and explanations for your opinions and plans.

Levels follow the CEFR (Common European Framework) — the same scale used by official exams like DELE.

The B1 lessons in the app

  1. Lesson 31: Por vs Para — Two little words, one clear rule
  2. Lesson 32: Two Pasts — Preterite vs Imperfect — What happened vs how it was
  3. Lesson 33: Gustar & Verbs Like It — Me gusta, me encanta, me duele...
  4. Lesson 34: Reflexive Verbs — My Day, Deep — Me levanto, me ducho, me acuesto...
  5. Lesson 35: Giving Opinions & Agreeing — Creo que sí, estoy de acuerdo...
  6. Lesson 36: Phone, WhatsApp & Internet Deep — Messages, calls and data — modern life
  7. Lesson 37: At the Embassy & Official Work — Documents, appointments and formal Spanish
  8. Lesson 38: Home Problems & Repairs — The tap leaks, the light fails — fix it in Spanish
  9. Lesson 39: Health Deep — Pharmacy & Symptoms — At the pharmacy: exactly what you need
  10. Lesson 40: My Life Story — Past + future mixed — tell YOUR story

What you can do after B1

  • Use por and para correctly — the pair that confuses everyone.
  • Tell stories with both Spanish pasts: what happened vs how things were.
  • Say what you like, love and what hurts with gustar-style verbs.
  • Describe your whole day with reflexive verbs.
  • Give opinions and agree or disagree naturally.
  • Handle the embassy, official documents, home repairs and the pharmacy — and narrate your own life story.

The B1 final exam & certificate

When you finish the B1 lessons, the app unlocks the B1 Final Exam. Pass it and you earn a B1 certificate inside the app — with a code and a QR that anyone can check on the verify page.

One honest note: this certificate records your achievement in the app. It is not an official DELE qualification — for that, see the DELE guide. What it does prove is real: you finished the level and passed its exam.
