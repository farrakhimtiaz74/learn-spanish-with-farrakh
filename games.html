Games — Khel Khel Mein Spanish

16 games — har game ek alag skill ki chhupi hui mashq hai.

Seekhna sirf parhna nahi hota. App mein 16 games hain — har game ek alag skill ki mashq hai: pehchan, spelling, sunna, jumla banana, tez soch. Sab games XP dete hain aur personal best save rakhte hain.

🃏 Word Match

Spanish lafz ko us ke maani wale card se jodo — ghalat jori par card palat jata hai. Roz-marra alfaz ki tez pehchan banti hai.

⏱️ Timed Quiz

Waqt ke khilaf race: sahi jawab, zyada score. Jo seekha hai woh dabao mein yaad karna aata hai.

🪢 Hangman

Harf andaza laga kar lafz mukammal karo — ghalat harf par tasveer banti hai. Spelling aur letter patterns seekhte ho.

🔍 Word Search

Harfon ke jaal mein chhupay Spanish alfaz dhoondo. Nazrain alfaz ki shaklein pehchanne lagti hain.

🧠 Memory Match

Cards ultay pare hain — joray yaad karke milao. Yaad-dasht ke saath alfaz bhi pakkay hote hain.

🎱 Word Bingo

Awaz suno aur sahi lafz wala khana mark karo — bingo line bante hi jeet. Sun kar pehchanna seekhte ho.

🖼️ Photo Quiz

Tasveer dekho, sahi Spanish lafz chuno. Alfaz ko cheezon se jorna — bina tarjume soche.

🏃 Word Race

Tez raftaar sawal-jawab ki race — personal best todne ki koshish mein revision khud ho jata hai.

🔗 Word Chain

Pichhle lafz ke aakhri harf se naya lafz banao. Alfaz ka zakheera dimagh mein ghumta hai.

🎲 Board Game

Board par aagay barho, har khane par sawal. Luck thora, lekin ghalti par sawal hi rokta hai.

🕵️ Mystery

Ek chhoti si kahani, teen mulzim — clues sun kar aur parh kar asal mujrim pakro. Samajh (comprehension) ki asal mashq.

🧱 Sentence Stack

Girte hue alfaaz ko sahi tartib mein jama karke jumla banao. Grammar ki tartib haathon se seekhte ho.

🎡 Spin & Answer

Pahiya ghumao, jo category aaye usi ka sawal. Surprise ki wajah se har topic taza rehta hai.

🏆 Tournament Week

Poora hafta apne results jama karo — hafta ke aakhir mein apni hi behtareen performance se muqabla.

👥 Pass & Play

Ek hi phone dost ya ghar wale ke saath baari baari khelo. Doosre ko haraane ke chakkar mein dono seekh jate hain.

🐍 Snake Words

Saanp ko sahi alfaaz ki taraf le jao — ghalat lafz khaane se game khatam. Tez soch, tez pehchan.

Note: Games mein istemal hone walay tamaam alfaz wohi hain jo lessons aur flashcards mein seekhte ho — game khelna asal mein chhupa hua revision hai.
