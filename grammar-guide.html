Spanish Grammar Guide

Saari grammar aik jagah — har mushkil topic ka aasan guide.

How grammar works in this app

  • One idea per lesson. Every one of the 60 lessons carries exactly one grammar point, explained in simple Roman Urdu with examples you can hear.
  • Grammar Reference. Inside the app, the full reference collects every topic in one place — your pocket grammar book.
  • Drills that build reflexes. Conjugation Drill, the Subjunctive Trainer and dedicated Ser vs Estar and Por vs Para trainers turn rules into automatic answers.
  • Weakness Doctor. When a quiz goes wrong, the app points you back to the exact lesson that fixes that rule.

The ten guides below are the web companions to those lessons — short, clear, and honest about the tricky bits.

The 10 grammar guides

Ser vs Estar

Two verbs for "to be": ser for what something IS (identity, origin, profession), estar for how or where it is right now.

Pehchan ke liye ser, haalat aur jagah ke liye estar.

Por vs Para

Both translate as "for" — por for reasons, exchanges and movement through; para for purpose, destinations and deadlines.

Wajah aur tabadlay par por, maqsad aur manzil par para.

The Subjunctive

The mood of wishes, doubts, emotions and "I want you to…" — used after quiero que, es importante que and friends.

Khwahish, shak aur farmayish ke jumlon mein aata hai.

Present Tense

What happens now and habitually: the regular -ar / -er / -ir patterns plus the key irregulars like ser, ir and tener.

Roz ke kaam aur aadat — hablo, comes, vive.

Past Tense

Two pasts that work as a team: the preterite for finished events, the imperfect for how things were and used to be.

Ho chuka kaam preterite, purana manzar imperfect.

Future & Conditional

What will happen (hablaré) and what would happen (hablaría) — plans, promises, politeness and daydreams.

Kal ki baat future, "kar leta / jata" wali baat conditional.

Reflexive Verbs

Actions you do to yourself — me levanto, te duchas, se acuesta — the verbs of your daily routine.

Jo kaam tum khud par karo: uthna, nahana, sona.

Gustar & Verbs Like It

Spanish says it backwards: me gusta el té means "tea pleases me". Encantar, doler and importar work the same way.

Pasand ulta banti hai — me gusta = mujhe pasand hai.

Spanish Numbers

From uno to 999,999: the patterns for tens, hundreds and thousands, plus prices, dates and phone numbers.

Ginti, qeematein, tareekhein — sab aik hi pattern.

Pronunciation Guide

Five pure vowels, the silent h, the rolled rr and the letter-by-letter sounds — read any Spanish word out loud, correctly.

Har harf ki awaz — parho ge to bol bhi lo ge.
