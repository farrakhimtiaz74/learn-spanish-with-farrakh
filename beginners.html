Why Spanish?

Spanish is one of the most spoken languages on Earth — the everyday language of Spain and most of Latin America, and spoken by hundreds of millions of people. It opens doors: travel, jobs, business, football, films and music.

It is also one of the friendliest big languages for a beginner. Spanish is written almost exactly the way it sounds — once you know the five vowel sounds, you can read any word out loud correctly. Compare that with English spelling, and you'll feel the difference in week one.

How the app starts you at zero

Lesson 1 assumes you know nothing — not even "hola". Every lesson has the same calm rhythm:

  • Words with meanings in English, Roman Urdu and Urdu script, each with a 🔊 recorded voice.
  • Sentences and a dialogue that put the words to work immediately.
  • One grammar idea, explained in simple Roman Urdu — never a wall of rules.
  • A short quiz so you know the lesson actually stuck.

Flashcards and Smart Review (spaced repetition) then bring each word back just before you would forget it, and speaking practice starts from day one — listen, repeat, and hear yourself improve.

The first 5 things to learn

1. Greetings — the door-opener

Hola (hello) · Buenos días (good morning) · Me llamo… (my name is…) · Mucho gusto (nice to meet you) · Gracias (thank you). Five phrases, and you can already be polite anywhere.

2. Numbers 1–10

uno, dos, tres, cuatro, cinco, seis, siete, ocho, nueve, diez. Prices, phone numbers, your age, the time — numbers are everywhere, and Lesson 2 takes you to 100.

3. Ser — the verb "to be"

yo soy (I am) · tú eres (you are) · él / ella es (he / she is). One real sentence on day one: Soy de Pakistán — "I am from Pakistan." (Full story on the Ser vs Estar page.)

4. Present-tense regular verbs

Most Spanish verbs follow three tidy patterns: hablar → yo hablo (I speak), comer → tú comes (you eat), vivir → él vive (he lives). Learn the pattern once and hundreds of verbs unlock. See the Present Tense guide.

5. The 100 most common words

A small core of words — gracias, por favor, sí, no, agua, casa, tiempo… — covers a huge share of everyday Spanish. The app's flashcards start with exactly these. Browse them on the Top 100 Words page.
