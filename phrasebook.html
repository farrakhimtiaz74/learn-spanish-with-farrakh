Survival Phrasebook — 300 Phrases

Mushkil waqt ke 300 tayyar jumlay — parho, suno, aur zaroorat par foran bolo.

This is the app's complete Survival Phrasebook: 300 ready-to-say phrases for the moments when you cannot afford to search for words — an emergency, a doctor, a lost passport, a broken tap. Every phrase has its English meaning and a Roman Urdu rendering, so Urdu speakers can read and say it at once. The phrases are grouped by situation below. One honest detail: the last 100 phrases repeat the advanced sets on purpose — the hardest phrases come back a second time for revision, exactly as they do in the app.
