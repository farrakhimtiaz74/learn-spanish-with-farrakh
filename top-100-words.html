Top 100 Spanish Words & Phrases

Sab se pehle ye 100 alfaz seekh lo — roz-marra ki Spanish ka darwaza yahin se khulta hai.

These are the 100 words and short phrases the app puts first — the ones that unlock everyday conversation fastest. They are grouped the same way as the Top 100 pack inside the app: 10 groups of 10. Learn one group a day and in under two weeks you can greet people, ask questions, order food, go shopping and handle the basics anywhere Spanish is spoken.
