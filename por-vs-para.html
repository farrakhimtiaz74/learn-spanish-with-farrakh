Por vs Para — Two Words for "For"

English ke "for" ke liye Spanish mein do lafz hain — por aur para. Qaaida aasan hai, bas ye guide parh lo.

The one-line rule

POR looks backwards — the cause, the reason, the route, the exchange.
PARA looks forwards — the goal, the destination, the deadline, the person it is for.

POR — cause, route, exchange

SpanishEnglish
El tren pasa por el túnel.The train goes through the tunnel. (through / along)
Mira por la ventana, por favor.Look through the window, please.
Caminamos por el parque cada mañana.We walk through the park every morning.
Estudié español por dos horas.I studied Spanish for two hours. (duration)
Leo el periódico por la mañana.I read the newspaper in the morning. (part of the day)
Pagué diez euros por el libro.I paid ten euros for the book. (exchange)
Gracias por tu ayuda.Thank you for your help. (always POR after gracias)
No salimos por la lluvia.We didn't go out because of the rain. (reason)
Llegó tarde por el tráfico.He arrived late because of the traffic.
Te llamo por teléfono luego.I'll call you by phone later. (by means of)
Hoy trabajo por mi hermano; él está enfermo.Today I'm working in place of my brother; he is ill. (on behalf of)

PARA — goal, destination, deadline

SpanishEnglish
Estudio español para viajar a España.I study Spanish in order to travel to Spain. (purpose + infinitive)
Necesito gafas para leer.I need glasses (in order) to read.
Mañana salimos para Madrid.Tomorrow we leave for Madrid. (destination)
Este tren va para Barcelona.This train goes to Barcelona.
La tarea es para el lunes.The homework is for Monday. (deadline)
Necesito el informe para mañana.I need the report by tomorrow.
Este regalo es para ti.This gift is for you. (recipient)
Compré flores para mi madre.I bought flowers for my mother.
Para mí, el español no es difícil.For me (in my opinion), Spanish is not difficult.
Trabaja para una empresa grande.He works for a big company. (employer)

Quick test — ask yourself

Question in your headAnswer
"Because of what? Through where? In exchange for what? For how long?"POR
"In order to do what? Going where? Ready by when? Meant for whom?"PARA

Common mistakes

  • ❌ Gracias para tu ayuda. → ✅ Gracias por tu ayuda. "Thanks for..." is always POR.
  • ❌ Estudié para dos horas. → ✅ Estudié por dos horas. A length of time = POR.
  • ❌ Salimos por Madrid mañana. → ✅ Salimos para Madrid. The place you are heading to = PARA. (POR Madrid would mean "through/via Madrid".)
  • ❌ Este regalo es por ti. → ✅ Este regalo es para ti. Something meant for a person = PARA.
