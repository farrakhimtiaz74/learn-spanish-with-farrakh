Study Tips — Roz 15 Minute, Sahi Tareeqa

Lambi parhai nahi — sahi tareeqa. Ye 6 tips isi app ke tools par bane hain.

Bari class, lambi lecture — in ki zaroorat nahi. Spanish roz ke 15 minute se aati hai, agar tareeqa sahi ho. Ye tips isi app ke tools ko istemal karke banaye gaye hain.

1️⃣ Roz 15 minute ka tareeqa

  • 5 minute: Smart Review — jo cards aaj due hain, pehle woh
  • 5 minute: ek naya lesson ya 10 naye flashcards
  • 5 minute: sunna ya bolna — ek kahani, ek clip, ya mic practice

App ka Daily Workout yehi teeno cheezein ek tayyar session mein jod deta hai — sochna hi nahi parta, bas shuru kar do.

2️⃣ Pehle suno, phir bolo

Har naye lafz par pehle 🔊 suno, phir zor se bolo. Urdu bolne walon ke liye Spanish ki awazein zyada mushkil nahi — zyada tar awazein milti julti hain. Jahan farq hai (j, rr, ñ) wahan Pronunciation Guide aur app ki Pronunciation Clinic madad karti hai.

3️⃣ Ghaltiyon se seekho — un se daro nahi

Ghalat jawab nuqsan nahi, data hai. App tumhari ghaltiyan jama karta hai: Mistake Quiz sirf unhi ki mashq karata hai, Weakness Doctor batata hai kis topic mein kamzori hai, aur exam ki ghaltiyan Exam Retry Bank mein jaati hain. Kamzor topic ka lesson Doctor seedha khol deta hai.

4️⃣ SRS par bharosa karo

Naya lafz aaj yaad hai — kal? SRS isi liye hai: card 1, 3, 7, 21, 45 din par wapas aata hai. Due review ko kabhi skip mat karo; naye cards rok do, review nahi. Review hi yaad ko pakka karta hai, naya seekhna sirf shuru karta hai.

5️⃣ Haftay ka goal chhota rakho

App mein weekly goal 20, 30 ya 50 (seekhe hue alfaz/lessons ki ginti) set hota hai. Shuru mein 20 rakho. Goal pura honay ki khushi agle haftay ki taqat hai — bara goal aur adhoora hafta sirf hosla torta hai.

6️⃣ Exam se pehle: sirf revision

Final exam se pehle naya kuch mat seekho. Retry Bank ki ghaltiyan dohrao, ek Mock do, aur level ke purane lessons ki grammar jhalak dekho. Exam ke din dimagh ko taza rakho — raat bhar ki parhai se behtar hai.

Aakhri baat: Ek din choot jaye to kuch nahi bigarta — streak freeze isi liye hai. Jo cheez bigaarti hai woh hai hafton ka rukna. 15 minute, roz, bas. Baaki app sambhal lega.
