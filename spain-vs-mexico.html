Spain vs Mexico Spanish

Ek hi zabaan, do andaz — Spain aur Mexico ke 30 farq yahan hain.

Spanish is one language with local flavours. A car is el coche in Madrid and el carro in Mexico City; a computer is el ordenador in Spain and la computadora across most of Latin America. Neither is wrong — but knowing both stops confusion. These are all 30 pairs from the app's Spain vs Mexico pack, with a Roman Urdu note explaining each difference.

Word🇪🇸 Spain🇲🇽 MexicoNote (Roman Urdu)
Car · Gaariel cocheel carroSpain mein gaari = coche, Mexico aur Latin America mein carro. 'Carro' Spain mein purani gaari/thaila-gaari lagta hai.
Computer · Computerel ordenadorla computadoraDono sahi hain — bas ilaqa alag hai. Portátil (laptop) dono jagah chalta hai.
Mobile phone · Mobileel móvilel celularSpain: móvil, Mexico: celular.
OK / Sure · Theek haivaleóraleSpain ka 'vale' har jagah samajh aata hai. Mexico ka 'órale' dostana aur wahan ka khaas lafz hai.
Juice · Juiceel zumoel jugoSpain mein juice = zumo, Mexico mein jugo. Spain mein 'jugo' gosht ke rus ko bhi kehte hain.
Potato · Aalula patatala papaMexico mein aalu = papa. Khayal rahe: 'el Papa' (bari P se) = the Pope, aur Spain mein aalu ki standard shakal 'patata' hai.
To drive · Gaari chalanaconducirmanejarSpain: conducir, Mexico: manejar. 'Manejar' ka matlab 'chalana/handle karna' bhi hota hai.
To take / grab · Lena / pakarnacogertomar⚠️ Mexico mein 'coger' bohat hi bura aur fohash lafz hai — wahan hamesha 'tomar' ya 'agarrar' kaho! Spain mein coger bilkul aam hai (bus pakarna: coger el autobús).
Glasses · Ainaklas gafaslos lentesSpain: gafas, Mexico: lentes. Dhoop ki ainak: gafas de sol / lentes de sol.
Flat / Apartment · Flatel pisoel departamentoSpain mein rehne ki jagah = piso (jo manzil par bhi istemal hota hai). Mexico: departamento.
Waiter · Waiterel camareroel meseroSpain: camarero, Mexico: mesero (mesa = table se bana).
Plumber · Plumberel fontaneroel plomeroSpain: fontanero, Mexico: plomero (plomo = lead/seesa, purani pipein seesay ki hoti theen).
Lift / Elevator · Liftel ascensorel elevadorDono samajh aate hain; Spain ascensor, Mexico elevador zyada aam hai.
Car park / Parking · Parkingel aparcamientoel estacionamientoSpain: aparcamiento (verb: aparcar), Mexico: estacionamiento (estacionar). Hotelon mein dono milte hain.
Car boot / Trunk · Diggiel maleterola cajuelaSpain: maletero, Mexico: cajuela.
Strawberry · Strawberryla fresala fresaDono mulkon mein EK hi lafz: fresa. Aise alfaaz dono jagah chalte hain. (Kuch ilaqon mein 'frutilla' bhi kehte hain.)
Bus · Busel autobúsel camión⚠️ Mexico mein shehri bus = camión! Lekin Spain mein 'camión' ka matlab TRUCK hai. Ghalat fehmi se bachna.
Cake · Cakela tartael pastelSpain mein birthday cake = tarta, Mexico mein pastel. Spain mein 'pastel' chhoti pastry ko bhi kehte hain.
Jacket · Jacketla chaquetala chamarraSpain: chaqueta, Mexico: chamarra. Halki jacket dono jagah 'chaqueta' samajh aati hai.
Trainers / Sneakers · Joggerslas zapatillas de deportelos tenisSpain: zapatillas (de deporte), Mexico: tenis — bilkul English 'tennis shoes' ki tarah.
Swimming pool · Poolla piscinala albercaSpain: piscina, Mexico: alberca.
Cool / Great · Zabardast¡qué guay!¡qué padre!Spain mein 'guay' = cool. Mexico mein '¡qué padre!' kehte hain — lafz 'baap' hai, lekin matlab 'zabardast'!
Lad / youngster · Larkael chavalel chamacoSpain: chaval, Mexico: chamaco. Dono dostana, ghair-rasmi alfaaz hain.
Right now · Abhiahora mismoahoritaMexico ka 'ahorita' mazaq mein mashhoor hai: matlab 'abhi' bhi ho sakta hai aur 'thori dair mein' bhi! Spain: ahora mismo = bilkul abhi.
To queue · Line mein khara honahacer colahacer filaSpain: hacer cola, Mexico: hacer fila. Dono ka matlab line mein lagna hai.
Fridge · Fridgeel frigoríficoel refrigeradorSpain: frigorífico ya 'nevera', Mexico: refrigerador (muhawra: 'refri').
Tap / Faucet · Nalkael grifola llaveSpain: grifo, Mexico: 'la llave (del agua)'. Llave ka pehla matlab 'chaabi' hai — Mexico mein paani wali chaabi!
Peanut · Moong phaliel cacahueteel cacahuateSpain: cacahuete, Mexico: cacahuate. Mamuli sa farq, dono samajh aate hain.
Green beans · Sabz phaliyanlas judías verdeslos ejotesSpain: judías verdes, Mexico: ejotes. Aam phali ko dono jagah 'judías/frijoles' kehte hain (khaas ilaqon mein farq hai).
To get angry · Naraz honaenfadarseenojarseDono sahi: Spain mein 'enfadarse' zyada, Mexico mein 'enojarse' zyada aam hai.
