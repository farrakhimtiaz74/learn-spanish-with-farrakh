Privacy Policy

Learn Spanish with Farrakh — Android app
Last updated: 9 November 2026

Short version

Ye app tumhari privacy ka pura khayal rakhti hai: koi ads nahi, koi trackers ya analytics nahi, koi data becha nahi jata, aur tumhari learning progress default tor par sirf tumhare phone ke andar rehti hai. Neeche poori tafseel hai.

In short: no ads, no trackers, no analytics, no data sold — your learning progress stays on your device unless you choose the optional cloud backup.

📴 The app works offline

Poora course — 60 lessons, 1,500 flashcards, recorded Spanish awaaz, exams aur games — app ke andar hi hai. Internet ke baghair sab kuch chalta hai. App tumhari koi browsing ya usage activity kisi server ko nahi bhejti.

💾 Your progress stays on your device

Tumhari progress (mukammal lessons, seekhe hue alfaaz, streak, scores, XP, settings, do profiles) phone ki local storage mein save hoti hai. Ye data khud-ba-khud kahin upload nahi hota. App uninstall karne se ye data phone se mit jata hai.

☁️ Optional cloud backup (your choice)

Agar tum chaho, to app ke andar email + password se account bana kar apni progress ka cloud backup rakh sakte ho (taaki phone badalne par progress wapas mil jaye). Ye bilkul optional hai — baghair account ke bhi poori app chalti hai.

  • Cloud mein sirf learning progress ka data jata hai (lessons, alfaaz, streak, scores, settings) — aur kuch nahi.
  • Backup service Supabase par chalta hai; har account ka data sirf usi account ko dikhta hai (row-level security).
  • Tumhara app-lock PIN aur us ka recovery code kabhi cloud mein nahi jata — wo sirf phone ke andar rehta hai.
  • App ke andar se sign out ya backup band kar sakte ho; cloud wali copy hatane ke liye neeche diye gaye contact par likho.

🎤 Microphone

Mic sirf tab istemal hota hai jab tum khud Speaking Practice mein mic button dabate ho, taaki tumhari awaaz Spanish se match ki ja sake (phone ki apni speech recognition se). Koi awaaz record karke kahin save ya upload nahi ki jati. Mic ki permission na do to bhi baqi poori app chalti hai.

📁 Backup files are yours

App se export ki gayi backup file (JSON/CSV/PDF) jahan tum save karo (phone storage, WhatsApp, Drive waghera), wo tumhari apni marzi aur zimmedari hai. Import bhi sirf tumhare chune hue file se hota hai.

🚫 What we never do

  • No advertisements — kabhi nahi.
  • No third-party trackers, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of personal data.
  • No account required to learn — sign-up sirf optional cloud backup ke liye hai.
  • Friends Board par sirf wohi naam aur progress publish hota hai jo tum khud showcase toggle on karke bhejte ho.

👶 Children

App har umar ke learners ke liye hai aur jaan-boojh kar bachon se koi personal data collect nahi karti; koi chat ya social feature ajnabiyon se rabta nahi karwata.
