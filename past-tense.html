Spanish Past Tense — Preterite vs Imperfect

Spanish mein guzra waqt kehne ke do tareeqay hain — ek mukammal kaam ke liye, ek pas-e-manzar ke liye. Farq samajh lo, aadhi mushkil khatam.

Two pasts, two jobs

PreteriteImperfect
JobA finished, completed action — the event itself.Background, habits and descriptions — how things were, what used to happen.
Signal wordsayer (yesterday), anoche, la semana pasada, en 2020siempre, cada día, de niño (as a child), mientras (while)
ExampleAyer fui al mercado. (Yesterday I went to the market — done.)De niño, vivía en un pueblo. (As a child I lived / used to live in a village.)

Often both appear together: the imperfect sets the scene, the preterite is what happened — Dormía cuando sonó el teléfono. (I was sleeping when the phone rang.)

Also from the app's data: poder → pude, pudiste, pudo… and saber → supe, supiste, supo… Note the pattern — irregular preterites have no written accents on the yo/él forms (fui, fue, tuve, tuvo), unlike regular ones (tomé, tomó).

Fun fact the app drills: ser and ir have identical preterite forms (fui, fuiste, fue…) — context tells you whether it means "was" or "went".

Imperfect — regular forms (much easier)

-ar verbs take -aba endings, -er/-ir verbs take -ía endings:

Personhablarcomervivir

Only three verbs are irregular in the imperfect: ser → era, ir → iba, ver → veía.

Examples in action

SpanishEnglish
Ayer fui al mercado.Yesterday I went to the market. (preterite — one finished trip)
La semana pasada estuve enfermo.Last week I was ill. (preterite — a closed period)
Ayer tomé té por la mañana.Yesterday I drank tea in the morning.
Cuando era niño, jugaba en la calle.When I was a child, I used to play in the street. (imperfect — habit)
Hacía sol y los niños jugaban fuera.It was sunny and the children were playing outside. (imperfect — scene)
Dormía cuando sonó el teléfono.I was sleeping when the phone rang. (imperfect scene + preterite event)

Common mistakes

  • Using the preterite for habits: ❌ De niño, fui a esa escuela cada día. → ✅ De niño, iba a esa escuela cada día. Repeated past = imperfect.
  • Adding accents to irregular preterites: ❌ Él tuvó → ✅ Él tuvo. Irregular stems carry no accent.
  • Confusing present and past twins: tomo = I drink (now) vs tomó = he/she drank. The accent is the whole difference — listen for it.
  • ❌ Yo hací la tarea. → ✅ Yo hice la tarea. Hacer's preterite stem is hic- (and hizo for él/ella).
