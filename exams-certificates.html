Exams & Certificates

Har level ka imtihan, aur pass karne par aisa certificate jo koi bhi verify kar sakay.

The exams inside the app

🎓 Final Exams — A1 to C1

Har level ke aakhir mein 20 sawalon ka final exam. Pass karne par us level ka certificate — A1 se C1 tak paanch certificates.

🇪🇸 DELE Mock Exam

Asal DELE pattern par bana hua practice mock — timer ke saath, taake asli imtihan se pehle mahaul ka andaza ho jaye.

🎧 Listening Exam

Sirf sun kar jawab do. Har clip sirf 2 dafa bajta hai — bilkul asal listening test ki tarah.

✍️ Writing Exam

Teen likhne ke prompts, har ek ke saath model answer — apni writing ko model se compare karo.

🎙️ Speaking Exam

Bol kar jawab dene wala exam — sawal suno, mic mein bolo, aur apni performance dekho.

🎯 Placement Test

Naye seekhne walon ke liye 2 minute ka test jo bata deta hai ke shuru kahan se karna hai — Lesson 1 se ya aagay se.

🔁 Exam Retry Bank

Jis sawal mein ghalti ho, woh Exam Retry Bank mein save ho jata hai. Baad mein sirf apni ghaltiyon ki mashq karo — bank 300 sawalon tak rakhta hai, taake kamzori hi tumhari practice ban jaye.

🏆 Certificates — code + QR ke saath

Pass karne par certificate banta hai jis par tumhara naam, level, score, tareekh aur ek unique code hota hai — saath mein ek QR code bhi. Koi bhi QR scan kare ya code Verify page par dale, website foran bata deti hai ke certificate asal hai ya nahi. Certificate ki shakal sample page par dekh sakte ho.

Sach baat: Ye certificates tumhari mehnat ka record hain — ye official DELE certificates nahi hain aur na hi koi official qualification. DELE ek alag, fees wala imtihan hai jo Instituto Cervantes leta hai. Hamari koshish hai ke is app ki practice tumhein us asal imtihan ke liye tayyar kare; tafseel DELE Guide mein hai.
