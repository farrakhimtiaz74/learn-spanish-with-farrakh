Cien vs ciento: exactly 100 on its own is cien (cien euros). As soon as anything follows, it becomes ciento: ciento uno (101), ciento quince (115). Watch the three rebels: quinientos (500, not *cincocientos), setecientos (700), novecientos (900).

Hundreds agree with the noun's gender: doscientos euros but doscientas casas (200 houses).

Mil never changes — no plural, no "un mil": 1,000 is simply mil, 100,000 is cien mil. One adjustment: a final uno becomes un (and veintiuno → veintiún) right before mil: veintiún mil (21,000), ciento un mil (101,000), treinta y un mil (31,000).

Numbers in prices

Spain writes prices the European way — €1.200,00 means one thousand two hundred euros (dot = thousands, comma = decimals). Spoken:

PriceYou say
€9,99nueve euros con noventa y nueve (céntimos)
€25,50veinticinco euros con cincuenta
€125,50ciento veinticinco euros con cincuenta
€1.200,00mil doscientos euros
€2.499,00dos mil cuatrocientos noventa y nueve euros

Common mistakes

  • ❌ un mil → ✅ mil. And 2,000 is dos mil, never "dos miles" for counting.
  • ❌ ciento euros for exactly 100 → ✅ cien euros; but ✅ ciento cinco euros (105).
  • ❌ cincocientos → ✅ quinientos (500). Same family: setecientos (700), novecientos (900).
  • ❌ Reading €1.200 as "one point two" — in Spanish prices the dot groups thousands: it is mil doscientos euros.
