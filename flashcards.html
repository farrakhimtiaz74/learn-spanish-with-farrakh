What is on every card?

Ek card, chaar cheezein

Har card par Spanish lafz, us ki recorded awaz (🔊 tap karo), English meaning, Roman Urdu meaning aur Urdu script meaning — teeno saath, taake tum jis script mein aram mehsoos karo usi mein seekho.

Har card ke saath ek example sentence bhi hai — lafz akela nahi, jumlay mein seekhte ho, aur us jumlay ki bhi apni recorded awaz hai.

SRS — bhoolne se pehle review

Jo card Maloom hai mark karo, woh dobara 1 din, phir 3, 7, 21 aur 45 din baad aata hai. Ye waqfa wohi hai jo yaad ko lamba rakhta hai. Smart Review har roz sirf wohi cards dikhata hai jo aaj due hain — na zyada, na kam.

Due cards ka count home-screen widget par bhi nazar aata hai, aur Daily Workout mein review khud shamil hota hai.

My Words aur search

Kisi card ko ⭐ pasandeeda banao to woh My Words mein save ho jata hai — apni mushkil ya pasandeeda alfaz ki list, PDF mein export bhi kar sakte ho. Search se koi bhi lafz — Spanish, English ya Urdu mein — foran dhoond sakte ho, packs aur grammar samet.

Tip: Roz sirf 10 naye cards + due review — ye chhota sa routine 1,500 alfaz tak pohoncha deta hai. Tafseel Study Tips mein hai.
