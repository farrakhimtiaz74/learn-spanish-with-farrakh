A1 Spanish — Beginner

Bilkul ibtida — salaam dua se pehli mukammal guftagu tak.

What A1 means

Beginner. You can understand and use familiar everyday expressions and very basic phrases, introduce yourself and others, and interact in a simple way when the other person speaks slowly and clearly.

Levels follow the CEFR (Common European Framework) — the same scale used by official exams like DELE.

The A1 lessons in the app

  1. Lesson 1: Greetings & Introductions — Hola, Buenos días, Me llamo...
  2. Lesson 2: Numbers 1–100 — Counting, prices and your age
  3. Lesson 3: Days, Months & Time — ¿Qué hora es? — What time is it?
  4. Lesson 4: Colours & Common Objects — Red, blue, big, small — describing things
  5. Lesson 5: Family & People — Talking about your family
  6. Lesson 6: Food & Restaurant — Ordering food like a local
  7. Lesson 7: Ser vs Estar — "To Be" — Two verbs, one simple rule
  8. Lesson 8: Present Tense Regular Verbs — -ar, -er, -ir verbs made easy
  9. Lesson 9: Asking Questions — qué, dónde, cuándo, cuánto...
  10. Lesson 10: Directions & Travel — Taxi, hotel, airport — getting around
  11. Lesson 11: Shopping & Money — Prices and polite bargaining
  12. Lesson 12: Daily Routine Verbs — Wake up, eat, work, sleep
  13. Lesson 13: Past Tense Basics — Yesterday I... — talking about the past
  14. Lesson 14: Future with "Voy a..." — I am going to... — easy future
  15. Lesson 15: Health & Emergency Phrases — Phrases that can really help you
  16. Lesson 16: Phone & Internet Words — Mobile, Wi-Fi and messages
  17. Lesson 17: Jobs & Work Talk — What do you do for work?
  18. Lesson 18: Feelings & Opinions — Me gusta, creo que... — saying what you think
  19. Lesson 19: Making Plans & Invitations — ¿Quieres...? — inviting friends
  20. Lesson 20: Full Conversation Review — Everything together — you can do this!

What you can do after A1

  • Greet people properly and introduce yourself and your family.
  • Use numbers up to 100 — prices, your age, phone numbers, the time.
  • Order food, shop politely and ask simple questions.
  • Ask for directions and handle basic travel situations.
  • Talk about your daily routine, your job, your feelings and your plans with "voy a…".
  • Say simple things about yesterday — and hold a short, friendly conversation (Lesson 20 puts it all together).

The A1 final exam & certificate

When you finish the A1 lessons, the app unlocks the A1 Final Exam. Pass it and you earn a A1 certificate inside the app — with a code and a QR that anyone can check on the verify page.

One honest note: this certificate records your achievement in the app. It is not an official DELE qualification — for that, see the DELE guide. What it does prove is real: you finished the level and passed its exam.
