About

Urdu bolne walon ke liye banayi gayi free Spanish app ki kahani.

Why this app exists

Most Spanish apps assume you already read English fluently, and many lock lessons behind a paywall or interrupt you with ads. Learn Spanish with Farrakh was built the other way around: every lesson is explained in Roman Urdu, everything is free, and the whole course lives inside the app — no internet needed.

It is an independent project, built with care for one audience first: Urdu speakers who want to learn Spanish from zero, at their own pace, on an ordinary Android phone.

What's inside

60

Lessons, A1 to C1 — a step-by-step course with words, sentences, dialogues, grammar and a quiz in every lesson.

1,500

Flashcards in 38 categories — every card with an example sentence and Smart Review (spaced repetition) so words stick.

7,902

Recorded audio clips — a real Spanish (es-ES) voice throughout the app, not a robot reading on the fly.

46

Verbs with full tables — present, past, future, conditional and subjunctive, including the tricky reflexive verbs.

20+

Games — Hangman, Snake Words, Word Race, Board Game, Mystery and more, plus XP, streaks and daily quests.

🏅

Exams & certificates — level exams, DELE-style mocks, listening and speaking exams. Every certificate carries a code that anyone can check on the verify page.

How it works

  • Offline first: lessons, cards, audio, exams and games are all inside the app. No signal? No problem.
  • Your progress stays yours: it is saved on your phone. An optional cloud backup (email + password) exists only if you want it.
  • Made for real phones: two learner profiles on one device, a PIN app lock, home-screen widgets and a daily reminder.
  • No ads, no paywall: free forever — that is a promise, not a trial.

Who it's for

Students, job seekers, travellers, and anyone who has always wanted to learn Spanish but found English-only apps hard to follow. If you can read Roman Urdu, you can start today — lesson 1 assumes nothing.
