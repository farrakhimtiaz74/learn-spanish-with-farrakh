B2 Spanish — Upper Intermediate

Ab tum behes, khabrein aur daftari Spanish bhi sambhal saktay ho.

What B2 means

Upper intermediate. You can interact with a degree of fluency and spontaneity, produce clear, detailed speech on a wide range of subjects, and argue for and against a point of view.

Levels follow the CEFR (Common European Framework) — the same scale used by official exams like DELE.

The B2 lessons in the app

  1. Lesson 41: Subjunctive — First Steps — Quiero que..., es importante que...
  2. Lesson 42: Conditional — Me gustaría… — I would like…, I would go…
  3. Lesson 43: Reported Speech — Dijo que… — telling what people said
  4. Lesson 44: Formal Letters & Emails — Estimado señor…, atentamente…
  5. Lesson 45: Understanding the News — Titulares, según informa…, en directo
  6. Lesson 46: Opinions, Debate & Arguing Politely — Estoy de acuerdo, pero…, en cambio…
  7. Lesson 47: Spanish for Work — Meetings & Calls — La reunión, el orden del día, quedamos en…
  8. Lesson 48: Culture, Festivals & Small Talk — La sobremesa, las fiestas, charlar…
  9. Lesson 49: If-Clauses — Si tengo tiempo…, si llueve…
  10. Lesson 50: My Spanish Story — Full Review — Everything from 1 to 50, in your own story

What you can do after B2

  • Use the subjunctive for wishes and requirements (quiero que…).
  • Say what you would do with the conditional (me gustaría…).
  • Report what other people said (dijo que…).
  • Write formal letters and emails that sound professional.
  • Follow the news and discuss it.
  • Debate politely, handle work meetings and calls, and use if-clauses with confidence.

The B2 final exam & certificate

When you finish the B2 lessons, the app unlocks the B2 Final Exam. Pass it and you earn a B2 certificate inside the app — with a code and a QR that anyone can check on the verify page.

One honest note: this certificate records your achievement in the app. It is not an official DELE qualification — for that, see the DELE guide. What it does prove is real: you finished the level and passed its exam.
