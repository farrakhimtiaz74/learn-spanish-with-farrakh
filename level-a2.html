A2 Spanish — Elementary

Roz-marra ki zindagi ke kaam ab Spanish mein ho jayenge.

What A2 means

Elementary. You can use sentences and common expressions about familiar, everyday areas — home, health, money, work — and handle simple, routine exchanges of information.

Levels follow the CEFR (Common European Framework) — the same scale used by official exams like DELE.

The A2 lessons in the app

  1. Lesson 21: Weather Talk — Small talk about the weather — very Spanish!
  2. Lesson 22: At the Doctor (deep) — Explaining exactly what hurts
  3. Lesson 23: Renting a House — Finding and renting a home
  4. Lesson 24: Bank & Money — Accounts, cards and changing money
  5. Lesson 25: Phone Call Practice — Saying it right on the phone
  6. Lesson 26: Celebrations & Guests — Birthdays, weddings and hosting guests
  7. Lesson 27: Complaints Politely — Fixing problems without a fight
  8. Lesson 28: My Village & City — Talking about where YOU live
  9. Lesson 29: Hopes & Dreams — Quiero, espero, un día... — your future
  10. Lesson 30: Story Telling — Past + future mixed — tell your story!

What you can do after A2

  • Make small talk about the weather like a local.
  • Explain to a doctor exactly what hurts.
  • Rent a house, deal with the bank and handle money matters.
  • Hold your own on a phone call in Spanish.
  • Host guests, celebrate, and make a complaint politely — without a fight.
  • Talk about your village or city, your hopes and dreams, and tell a story mixing past and future.

The A2 final exam & certificate

When you finish the A2 lessons, the app unlocks the A2 Final Exam. Pass it and you earn a A2 certificate inside the app — with a code and a QR that anyone can check on the verify page.

One honest note: this certificate records your achievement in the app. It is not an official DELE qualification — for that, see the DELE guide. What it does prove is real: you finished the level and passed its exam.
