FAQ — Frequently Asked Questions

Aksar poochay jane walay sawalon ke seedhay jawab.

Is the app really free?

Yes. Every lesson, flashcard, exam and game is free. There is no paid version, no in-app purchases and no locked content.

Are there any ads?

No. The app has no ads at all, and it never will. The website has no ads either.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The whole course — 60 lessons, 1,500 flashcards, 7,902 recorded audio clips, exams and games — is inside the app. You only need internet if you choose the optional cloud backup, or to download an update.

Do I need an account or sign-up?

No. The app works fully without any account. An email + password account exists only for the optional cloud backup of your progress.

Is the app on the Google Play Store?

Not yet. The Play Store package (.aab) is built and ready, but publishing needs a Google Play developer account. Until then, download the official Android APK free from GitHub (see the Download page).

What about iPhone?

There is no iOS app yet. On an iPhone you can use the free web version of the app in your browser — it has the same 60 lessons and 1,500 flashcards.

Which phones does it support?

Any Android phone running Android 5.0 or newer. The download is about 32 MB.

How do I update the app?

Download the newest APK from the Releases page and install it over the old app. Every version is signed with the same key, so your progress carries over. The app also checks for updates itself.

I forgot my app-lock PIN. What can I do?

When you set a PIN, the app shows you a one-time recovery code. Enter that code on the lock screen to remove the PIN and set a new one. Your PIN and recovery code never leave your phone — they are not part of the cloud backup.

What is cloud backup, and is it safe to skip?

Cloud backup is optional. With an email + password account, only your learning progress (lessons done, words learned, scores, settings) is backed up through Supabase so you can restore it on a new phone. Your PIN never goes to the cloud. Skipping it changes nothing about learning.

What happens to my progress if I change phones?

Use the cloud backup, or export a backup file from the app (you can protect it with a password) and import it on the new phone. Without one of these, progress stays on the old phone — it is never uploaded automatically.

Can two people learn on one phone?

Yes. The app has two separate profiles, each with its own lessons, words, scores — and its own PIN and cloud account if you want.

Are the certificates official qualifications?

No. They are records of your learning achievement inside the app, each with a code that can be verified on this website. They are not DELE certificates and not official qualifications — we say this plainly so nobody is misled.

Does the app teach Spain Spanish or Mexican Spanish?

The recorded voice and main course use Spain Spanish (es-ES). A dedicated 'Spain vs Mexico' pack inside the app shows where words and usage differ, so you can understand both.

What is the Urdu script beta?

An optional setting that shows the bottom dock and main headings in Urdu script. The rest of the app stays in Roman Urdu for now — it is labelled beta honestly, and you can switch it off any time.

What data does the app collect about me?

None. No trackers, no analytics, no ads SDKs. Your progress stays on your device unless you turn on the optional cloud backup. The full detail is on the Privacy page.
