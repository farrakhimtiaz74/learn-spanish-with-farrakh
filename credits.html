🖼️ Photos — Openverse (Creative Commons)

Lesson photos are real photographs under Creative Commons licences, found via Openverse. The full per-photo credit list (photographer, licence and source for every image) ships inside the app in its CREDITS file. The C1 lesson photos, for example:

🔊 Voice — Microsoft Edge neural TTS

The app's 7,902 Spanish recordings were generated with Microsoft Edge's neural text-to-speech, using a Spanish (es-ES) voice, then trimmed and optimised so the whole course still fits in a small offline app.

🔤 Fonts — Outfit & Plus Jakarta Sans

The app's typefaces are Outfit and Plus Jakarta Sans, distributed via Fontsource under the SIL Open Font License (OFL).

📷 QR codes — ZXing

Certificate QR codes are generated with the ZXing library ("Zebra Crossing"), used under the Apache License 2.0.

☁️ Cloud backup — Supabase

The optional progress backup runs on Supabase. GitHub Pages hosts this website, and GitHub Releases distribute the app.

✍️ Content

The lessons, flashcards, translations (English and Roman Urdu), example sentences, stories and exercises were written for this app. Public-domain poems and songs used in the poetry and music sections are credited on their own screens in the app.
