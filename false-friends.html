False Friends — Words That Trick You

Ye alfaz English lagtay hain magar matlab bilkul aur hai — dhoka mat khana.

False friends are traps: Spanish words that look like English words you know — but mean something completely different. Say embarazada and you have not said "embarrassed"; you have said "pregnant". Here are all 24 false friends from the app's pack: the word, the English word it looks like, what it really means, and a Roman Urdu note so the trap never catches you.
