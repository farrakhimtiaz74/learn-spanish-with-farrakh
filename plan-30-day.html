The 30-Day Spanish Plan

Ek mahina, roz sirf 15–20 minute — aur pehla exam pass.

The daily routine — 15 to 20 minutes

StepTimeWhat to do
📖 1 lesson~10 minThe next lesson in order. Read, listen to every line, take its quiz.
🃏 20 cards~5 minFlashcards + Smart Review — it resurfaces words right before you'd forget them.
🎤 Speak~5 minListen-and-repeat, or the speaking practice. Out loud — whispering doesn't count!

Thora roz, roz. Twenty quiet minutes daily beats three hours once a week — your memory files words away between sessions, not during them.

Week 1 (Days 1–7) — The foundations

Lessons 1–7 + saying your greetings out loud every day.

  • Greetings & introductions, numbers 1–100, days, months and the time
  • Colours and describing things, family, food and ordering at a restaurant
  • Lesson 7: ser vs estar — the most important early grammar idea

By Sunday: you can introduce yourself, count, tell the time and order food. (Day-by-day detail: the beginners guide.)

Week 2 (Days 8–14) — Moving around

Lessons 8–14 + flashcards every day (this is when Smart Review starts earning its keep).

  • Present-tense regular verbs and asking questions properly
  • Directions and travel, shopping and money, your daily routine
  • Lesson 13: past tense basics — "yesterday I…"; Lesson 14: the easy future, "voy a…"

By Sunday: you can get around a town, buy things, and talk about your day — past, present and tomorrow.

Week 3 (Days 15–21) — Real life kicks in

Lessons 15–21 + the daily 5 minutes of speaking becomes non-negotiable this week.

  • Health and emergency phrases, phone and internet words, jobs and work talk
  • Feelings and opinions (me gusta…), making plans and invitations
  • Lesson 20: the full conversation review — everything together, in one flowing chat
  • Lesson 21 opens A2: weather small talk, the Spanish way

By Sunday: you can hold a real, simple conversation and handle the topics daily life actually throws at you.

Week 4 (Days 22–30) — A2, and your first exam

Lessons 22–30, then the big finish.

  • At the doctor (in depth), renting a house, bank and money, phone-call practice
  • Celebrations and guests, polite complaints, talking about your village or city
  • Hopes and dreams, and Lesson 30: storytelling — your story, in Spanish
  • Day ~30: sit the A2 Final Exam in the app and earn your certificate (verify it on the verify page)

By day 30: you have finished the whole A1+A2 path — 30 lessons — with an exam pass to show for it.

Honest expectations — koi jhooti umeed nahi

  • After 30 days you will handle everyday basics: greetings, food, travel, family, health, simple stories. That is genuinely A1–A2 Spanish.
  • You will not be fluent, and nobody honestly can be in a month. Fluency is this same routine, continued for months — the app goes all the way to C1 when you're ready.
  • Fast Spanish on TV will still sound fast. Slow, clear Spanish on familiar topics will start making sense — that's the real milestone.
  • Missed a day? Kuch nahi hota. The plan bends; just pick up the next lesson. The app even keeps streak freezes for exactly this.
