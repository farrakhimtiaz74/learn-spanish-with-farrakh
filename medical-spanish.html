Medical Spanish — 30 Phrases

Doctor ko apni takleef Spanish mein samjhao — dard, bukhaar, dawa, sab.

When you are ill in a Spanish-speaking country, the right sentence matters more than perfect grammar. These 30 phrases from the app's Medical pack were reviewed line by line: they use the polite usted form with doctors and cover symptoms, pain, medicines, the pharmacy and asking for help — so you can say exactly what hurts.

SpanishEnglishRoman Urdu
Me duele la cabeza.My head hurts.Mere sar mein dard hai.
Me duele la garganta.My throat hurts.Mere galay mein dard hai.
Me duelen los pies.My feet hurt.Mere paon mein dard hai.
Tengo fiebre.I have a fever.Mujhe bukhaar hai.
Tengo tos y estoy resfriado.I have a cough and a cold.Mujhe khaansi aur zukaam hai.
Soy alérgico a la penicilina.I'm allergic to penicillin.Mujhe penicillin se allergy hai.
Soy diabético y necesito insulina.I'm diabetic and I need insulin.Mujhe sugar (diabetes) hai aur insulin chahiye.
Necesito un médico que hable inglés.I need a doctor who speaks English.Mujhe aisa doctor chahiye jo English bolta ho.
Es una emergencia: llame a una ambulancia.It's an emergency: call an ambulance.Ye emergency hai: ambulance bulayein.
Me he roto el brazo.I've broken my arm.Mera bazu toot gaya hai.
Me he quemado con agua caliente.I burned myself with hot water.Main garam paani se jal gaya hoon.
Me siento mareado.I feel dizzy.Mujhe chakkar aa rahe hain.
Tengo diarrea desde ayer.I've had diarrhoea since yesterday.Mujhe kal se dast (diarrhoea) hain.
Tome esta pastilla cada ocho horas.Take this pill every eight hours.Ye goli har aath (8) ghante mein lein. (doctor ki hidayat)
Hay que tomarla con comida.It must be taken with food.Ye dawa khane ke saath leni hai.
Respire hondo, por favor.Breathe deeply, please.Baraye meherbani gehra saans lein.
Abra la boca y diga «ah».Open your mouth and say 'ah'.Munh kholein aur 'aa' kahein.
Necesita reposo durante una semana.You need rest for a week.Aap ko ek hafta aaram chahiye.
¿Desde cuándo tiene estos síntomas?Since when have you had these symptoms?Ye alaamaat kab se hain?
¿Tiene seguro médico?Do you have medical insurance?Kya aap ke paas medical insurance hai?
Me torcí el tobillo jugando al fútbol.I twisted my ankle playing football.Football khelte hue mera takhna mur gaya.
Me duele una muela.I have toothache (a molar hurts).Meri daarh mein dard hai.
¿Puede darme algo para el dolor?Can you give me something for the pain?Kya aap mujhe dard ki dawa de sakte hain?
Sin receta no puedo vender este medicamento.Without a prescription I can't sell this medicine.Parchi ke baghair main ye dawa nahi bech sakta. (pharmacy wala kehta hai)
La farmacia de guardia está abierta toda la noche.The duty pharmacy is open all night.Duty wali pharmacy poori raat khuli hai.
Tengo la tensión alta.I have high blood pressure.Mera blood pressure zyada hai.
Creo que es una intoxicación alimentaria.I think it's food poisoning.Mera khayal hai ye khane ki kharaabi (food poisoning) hai.
Hay que hacerle un análisis de sangre.We need to do a blood test on you.Aap ka khoon ka test karna hoga.
No es grave, pero necesita cuidados.It's not serious, but it needs care.Ye khatarnaak nahi, lekin dekh bhaal chahiye.
Llámeme si empeora.Call me if it gets worse.Agar halat bigray to mujhe phone karein.
