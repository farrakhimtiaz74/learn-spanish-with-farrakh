DELE Exam Guide

DELE asal imtihan hai — app uski tayyari ki jagah hai, certificate uska badal nahi.

What DELE is

DELE (Diplomas de Español como Lengua Extranjera) is the official Spanish language qualification. The diplomas are issued by the Instituto Cervantes on behalf of Spain's Ministry of Education, and they are recognised worldwide by employers, universities and governments. If a job or a university asks you to "prove your Spanish", DELE is usually what they mean.

Exams are held at official test centres on set dates during the year, and there is an exam fee. You register through the Instituto Cervantes and sit the exam in person.

📢 Said plainly: our certificates are NOT DELE

The certificates inside Learn Spanish with Farrakh record your learning achievement in the app — you finished a level and passed its exam, and anyone can check the code on the verify page. They are not DELE certificates and not official qualifications, and we will never pretend otherwise. Think of the app as your training ground; DELE is the official match.

The four exam parts

Every DELE level tests the same four skills. You need to pass the exam as a whole — strong speaking can't fully rescue a failed listening paper.

PartWhat happens
Reading
(Comprensión de lectura)		Texts — notices, emails, articles — with questions on what they say and what they mean.
Listening
(Comprensión auditiva)		Recorded conversations and announcements, played under exam conditions, with questions.
Writing
(Expresión escrita)		Real tasks: a message, a letter, an opinion text — correct, clear and on-topic.
Speaking
(Expresión oral)		A conversation with examiners: describing, narrating, giving opinions and reacting to prompts.

How the app prepares you

  • DELE Mock exam — a full timed practice exam inside the app in the same spirit as the real one, so the format never surprises you.
  • Level final exams — one per level, A1 to C1, so you always know which level you really are.
  • Listening Exam — recorded Spain-Spanish audio with a strict two-play rule, just like exam discipline.
  • Speaking Exam & Conversation Simulator — practise answering out loud before a human ever asks.
  • Writing Exam — prompts with model answers, so you learn what a good text looks like.
  • Exam Retry Bank & Mistake Quiz — every question you miss comes back until it sticks.

Study advice

  • Finish the lessons of your target level first — the mock exam then tells the truth about your readiness.
  • Take mocks under real timing, in one sitting, phone on silent. Exam stamina is a skill of its own.
  • Speak a little every day (5 minutes counts). Speaking is the part candidates most often leave too late.
  • Register early — centres and dates fill up, and the official dates are published on the Instituto Cervantes website.
  • Keep it in proportion: the app + regular practice gets your Spanish ready; the certificate is just the proof.
